## What you may do

You are welcome to:

- **Link** to any page on the site without asking.
- **Quote** short passages, up to about 100 words, with a clear credit and a link to the original page.
- **Reference our figures** in your own work with a link to the guide they came from.
- **Share** our guides on social media and in newsletters using the page link.

A suitable credit looks like this: Source: CodeNexon, followed by a link to the page.

## What needs permission

Please ask first if you want to:

- Republish a full article or most of one, in any language.
- Use our content in a paid product, course or printed publication.
- Reproduce our tables or images beyond a short quotation.
- Use our content to train or fine-tune a commercial machine learning model.

Send requests to [info@codenexon.com](mailto:info@codenexon.com) with the page address and how you plan to use it.

## Reporting a copyright concern

If you believe something on the site infringes your copyright, email [info@codenexon.com](mailto:info@codenexon.com) with:

1. Your name and contact details.
2. A description of the work you own.
3. The address of the page on our site where the material appears.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized.
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

We review every notice and will remove or correct material where the claim is valid.

## If your content was removed by mistake

If we removed something following a complaint and you believe that was an error, write to the same address explaining why. We will review it again.
