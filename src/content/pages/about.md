CodeNexon publishes plain-English guides and comparisons for people who build and run websites and small online businesses. We cover three areas: [hosting and cloud platforms](/hosting-cloud), [WordPress](/wordpress), and the [software and tools](/saas-tools) a small business depends on.

## What we are here to do

Choosing a web host, moving a site or picking an email tool should not need a week of research. Most of the answers already exist on vendor pricing pages and in documentation. They are just scattered, written in sales language, and out of date within months.

Our job is to gather those facts, check them against the source, do the arithmetic, and say clearly what they mean for someone about to spend money or change a working website.

## The rules every guide follows

- **The answer comes first.** You should know our view from the first paragraph, with the detail below for anyone who wants it.
- **Every price has a date.** Figures are taken from the vendor's own page and marked with the day they were checked.
- **Sources are linked.** Each guide ends with the pages it relied on.
- **The math is shown.** When we say a plan costs $911.40 over five years, the guide shows how we got there.
- **No claimed tests that did not happen.** If a guide is based on published information, it says so near the top.
- **Downsides are stated.** A recommendation without its trade-off is an advertisement.

The full method is on [How We Test and Research](/how-we-test), and the standards behind it are in our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy).
