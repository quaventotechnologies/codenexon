The quickest way to reach CodeNexon is by email at [info@codenexon.com](mailto:info@codenexon.com). Every message is read.

## What to write to us about

| Reason | What helps us respond quickly |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| A correction | The page address, the figure or step that is wrong, and a link to the correct source if you have one |
| A question about a guide | The page address and what you are trying to do |
| A topic suggestion | The question you want answered and who it is for |
| A copyright concern | The details listed in our [Copyright Policy](/copyright-policy) |
| A privacy request | What you would like us to show, correct or delete. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) |
| A business enquiry | Who you are, what you propose, and that you have read our [Disclosure](/disclosure) rules |

## Corrections come first

Prices and plan details change often, and readers frequently spot a change before we do. If something on the site is out of date, tell us. We check it against the vendor's page, fix the guide and record the change in its update history.

## What we cannot help with

- **Individual technical support.** We cannot log in to your server or debug a specific site. Your hosting provider's support team is the right place for that.
- **Account or billing problems with a vendor.** We are not affiliated with the companies we write about and have no access to your accounts.
- **Paid placements.** We do not sell rankings, reviews or links.
