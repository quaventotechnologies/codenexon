Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: September 2024

CodeNexon believes in full transparency. This page explains our affiliate relationships and how they work.

What is an Affiliate Link?

An affiliate link is a special tracked URL. When you click an affiliate link on CodeNexon and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or not.

Our Affiliate Relationships

CodeNexon participates in affiliate programmes including:

  • Amazon Associates — Amazon India and Amazon.com affiliate programme
  • Flipkart Affiliate Programme
  • Other brand-specific affiliate programmes as disclosed in individual articles

How It Works in Practice

When you see phrases like "Buy on Amazon" or "Check Price on Flipkart" in our articles, these are typically affiliate links. We clearly identify these where possible.

Does This Affect Our Reviews?

No. Affiliate commissions do not influence our editorial judgement. We recommend products based on their merit, not based on which products earn us higher commissions. We regularly recommend products that earn us no commission at all when they are the best option for our readers.

Our editorial standards require that all recommendations are honest and based on genuine assessment. We never accept payment to recommend a specific product.

Sponsored Content

When content is sponsored by a brand, it is clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or "Advertorial" at the top of the article. Sponsored content is kept separate from our independent editorial content.

Questions?

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or sponsorship policy, please contact us.
