Top Earphones Under Rs 2000: Best Value Picks in 2024

Four wired and wireless earphones compared on battery life, sound quality, and features, based on published specifications, all priced under ₹2000.

Shubham Handore Published 15 Sep, 2026 ~4 min read

You don't need to spend a fortune for good audio. These earphones deliver genuinely impressive sound quality under Rs 2000, whether you want wired reliability or true wireless convenience.

Quick answer: boAt Airdopes 141 for battery life, realme Buds Q2 for sound quality and ANC, Zebronics Zeb-Sound Bomb Pro 2 for value, or Boat Bassheads 100 if you'd rather skip Bluetooth.
In this guide:
  • boAt Airdopes 141
  • realme Buds Q2
  • Zebronics Zeb-Sound Bomb Pro 2
  • Boat Bassheads 100
  • Which Should You Buy?
  • FAQs

Best Overall: boAt Airdopes 141

The Airdopes 141 pairs a class-leading 42 hours of total battery life (earbuds plus charging case) with Bluetooth 5.1 for a stable connection and low latency. The sound signature is bass-forward and punchy, which suits most casual listeners and works well for workouts. The earbuds are lightweight and comfortable enough for extended wear, and touch controls handle playback, calls, and voice assistant access without reaching for your phone.

Best Sound Quality: realme Buds Q2

The Buds Q2 uses 10mm bass boost drivers and includes active noise cancellation, a feature rarely found at this price point. The overall sound is more balanced than the boAt option, with noticeably better instrument separation, making it the better pick if you care about audio fidelity over pure bass impact. Call quality also benefits from the ANC hardware's dual-mic setup.

Best Value TWS: Zebronics Zeb-Sound Bomb Pro 2

Zebronics undercuts the competition on price while still packing in 40 hours of total battery life, responsive touch controls, and IPX5 water resistance for sweat and light rain protection, features you'd normally expect to pay more for. Sound quality is solid rather than exceptional, but for the price, it's the strongest value pick of the three.

Wired Alternative: Boat Bassheads 100

If you'd rather avoid charging earbuds altogether, wired options remain relevant, no battery anxiety, lower latency for gaming, and generally better sound-per-rupee since there's no Bluetooth chipset or battery to pay for. The Boat Bassheads 100 is a reliable budget wired pick with a tangle-resistant cable and an in-line mic for calls.

Which Should You Buy?

Choose the boAt Airdopes 141 for the longest battery life and all-day comfort. Choose the realme Buds Q2 if sound quality and ANC matter more to you than raw battery numbers. Choose the Zebronics Zeb-Sound Bomb Pro 2 if you want the most features for the lowest price, or a wired option like the Boat Bassheads 100 if you'd rather skip Bluetooth altogether.

FAQs

Is Bluetooth 5.1 fast enough for gaming?
For casual mobile gaming, yes; for competitive gaming where latency matters most, a wired option like the Bassheads 100 has the edge.

Do any of these support fast charging?
The boAt and Zebronics cases both offer quick top-up charging (roughly 10 minutes for a few hours of playback), check the current listing for exact figures.

Are these safe to use for calls?
Yes. All four options include a microphone, with the realme Buds Q2's dual-mic ANC setup rated as the clearest for calls.
