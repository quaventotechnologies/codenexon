Best Smartphones of 2024: Top Picks Across Every Budget

Six smartphones compared across every Indian price band, from sub-₹15,000 to flagship, based on published specifications and pricing as of September 2024.

Shubham Handore Published 04 Sep, 2026 ~7 min read

Whether you need a flagship powerhouse or a reliable budget phone for daily use, this guide covers the best smartphones available in India in 2024, across every price range. All prices are Indian market prices as of September 2024.

Quick answer: iPhone 15 Pro or Galaxy S24 Ultra for flagship buyers, OnePlus 12 for the best mid-flagship value, Redmi Note 13 Pro+ for the best camera under ₹30,000, and Poco X6 Neo under ₹15,000.

1. Best Premium iPhone, Apple iPhone 15 Pro (₹1,34,900)

The iPhone 15 Pro remains the benchmark for premium smartphones in India. Key highlights for Indian buyers:

  • A17 Pro chip: the fastest mobile chip available, handles everything effortlessly including heavy gaming and video editing
  • USB-C charging: finally switched from Lightning, works with standard chargers
  • 48MP main camera: excellent in all conditions; video quality is the best on any smartphone
  • Titanium build: significantly lighter than the stainless steel Pro models
  • Available on: Apple India Store, Amazon India, Flipkart, check for bank offer discounts of ₹5,000–8,000 on HDFC/ICICI cards

Drawback: No charger in box. You will need to purchase a USB-C adapter separately.

2. Best Android Flagship, Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra (₹1,29,999)

The Galaxy S24 Ultra is Samsung's most complete flagship and the best Android phone available in India today.

  • 200MP main camera: extraordinary detail; zoom photography is unmatched
  • Built-in S Pen: unique to the Ultra; valuable for note-takers, designers, and students
  • Galaxy AI: Circle to Search, Live Translate, and Note Assist work well and are genuinely useful
  • 5000mAh battery: reliable two-day battery for most Indian users
  • Available on: Samsung India, Amazon India, Flipkart, watch for launch offers with Samsung-specific bank EMI plans

3. Best Mid-Flagship, OnePlus 12 (₹64,999)

The OnePlus 12 is the smartest buy for Indian users who want near-flagship performance without the flagship price tag.

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3: same chip as Samsung's top-end flagships
  • Hasselblad camera tuning: natural, accurate colour reproduction
  • 100W SUPERVOOC charging: 0 to 100% in approximately 26 minutes; outstanding for India's charging habits
  • OxygenOS 14: clean, fast, near-stock Android experience
  • Available on: OnePlus India, Amazon India

4. Best Mid-Range, Samsung Galaxy A55 5G (₹37,999)

The Galaxy A55 5G delivers a premium experience at a mid-range price, a common requirement among Indian college students and young professionals.

  • IP67 water resistance: rare at this price point in India
  • 50MP OIS camera: stabilised main camera; reliably good photos in daylight
  • Samsung's 4-year OS update commitment: excellent long-term value
  • 5000mAh battery: consistent all-day battery life

5. Best Camera Phone Under ₹30,000, Redmi Note 13 Pro+ (₹26,999)

For Indian students and budget buyers who prioritise camera quality, the Redmi Note 13 Pro+ is outstanding value.

  • 200MP main camera: exceptional detail for the price; best camera in this segment
  • IP68 water & dust resistance: premium protection at an affordable price
  • 67W fast charging: charges fully in about 45 minutes
  • MIUI 14 on Android 13: feature-rich but can feel cluttered for some users
  • Available on: Mi India, Amazon India, Flipkart

6. Best Budget Phone, Poco X6 Neo (₹14,999)

If your budget is under ₹15,000, the Poco X6 Neo offers the best performance in this segment for Indian buyers.

  • MediaTek Dimensity 6080: capable processor for daily tasks, social media, and casual gaming
  • 5G support: future-ready connectivity on Jio and Airtel 5G networks
  • 5000mAh battery: reliable all-day battery life
  • 33W fast charging: adequate charging speed at this price

How to Choose the Right Smartphone for You

Use this framework before buying:

  • Budget under ₹15,000: Poco X6 Neo or Redmi 13C 5G
  • Budget ₹15,000–30,000: Redmi Note 13 Pro+ or Samsung Galaxy A35
  • Budget ₹30,000–70,000: Samsung Galaxy A55 or OnePlus 12R
  • Budget above ₹70,000: OnePlus 12, iPhone 15 Pro, or Samsung S24 Ultra
  • For students: OnePlus 12R (₹39,999) or Redmi Note 13 Pro+, best balance of features and value
  • For business use: Samsung Galaxy S24 (Galaxy AI features) or iPhone 15 Pro (best privacy)

Where to Buy in India

Compare prices across Amazon India, Flipkart, and brand official stores before purchasing. Bank card offers (HDFC, ICICI, SBI) frequently provide ₹3,000–10,000 discounts during sale events like Big Billion Days and Great Indian Festival. Always verify the seller rating before purchasing.

Warranty Note: All smartphones listed above come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty in India. Samsung and Apple offer extended warranty programs (Samsung Care+ and AppleCare+) available at purchase.

FAQs

Are these prices likely to change?
Yes. Indian smartphone pricing shifts frequently around sale events; treat the prices above as a September 2024 reference point and verify current pricing before buying.

Should I buy from a brand store or Amazon/Flipkart?
Either is generally safe for major brands; compare bank card offers and check seller ratings on marketplaces before choosing.

Is it worth buying last year's flagship instead?
Often yes, last year's flagship at a discount frequently outperforms this year's mid-range phone at the same price.
