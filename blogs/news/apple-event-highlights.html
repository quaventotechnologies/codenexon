Apple September 2024 Event: iPhone 16, AirPods 4, Watch Series 10

Everything Apple announced at its September 2024 event, and why the new hardware centers on enabling Apple Intelligence.

Shubham Handore Published 01 Sep, 2026 ~4 min read

Event held: 9 Sep, 2024. All features described below were announced at that event; availability dates are noted separately in each section.

Apple held its annual September event revealing its latest hardware lineup, headlined by the first iPhones built specifically for Apple Intelligence. Here's everything announced.

Quick answer: Apple announced the iPhone 16 lineup (with Apple Intelligence support), AirPods 4 (with ANC on select models), and the Apple Watch Series 10 (with FDA-cleared sleep apnea detection).

iPhone 16 and 16 Plus

The new A18 chip brings Apple Intelligence AI features to the standard model for the first time, previously AI features had been reserved for Pro-tier hardware. A new dedicated Camera Control button gives a hardware shortcut for adjusting exposure, zoom, and capturing photos without touching the screen. Apple also reports significantly improved battery life over the iPhone 15 generation, driven by the more efficient A18 chip, this is Apple's own claim, not independently verified by CodeNexon.

iPhone 16 Pro and Pro Max

The Pro models get a larger 6.3-inch display (6.9-inch on the Pro Max), the more powerful A18 Pro chip, and an improved 48MP ultrawide camera that now supports autofocus, useful for macro photography, which previously required switching lenses. The Pro Max in particular is positioned around battery life, with Apple claiming up to two days of use on a single charge for lighter usage patterns.

AirPods 4

AirPods 4 come in two variants: a standard model and one with active noise cancellation (ANC), the first time ANC has been available outside the AirPods Pro line. A new H2 chip improves audio processing and voice isolation on calls. The design moves away from silicone ear tips toward a more open-fit design, and USB-C charging is now standard across the lineup, matching the rest of Apple's product range.

Apple Watch Series 10

The Series 10 is Apple's thinnest watch yet at 9.7mm, paired with a larger display than previous generations for easier readability. The standout new health feature is FDA-cleared sleep apnea detection, which monitors breathing disturbances during sleep and can flag signs of moderate to severe sleep apnea for users to discuss with a doctor.

Availability and Who's Affected

All four product lines were announced at the 9 September 2024 event; Apple opened preorders shortly after the event, with general availability following about a week later, check Apple's official store for the exact date in your region. Apple Intelligence itself is a separate, staggered software rollout: it requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer (or the iPhone 16 lineup), and Apple has said it will arrive first in US English before expanding to other languages and regions, so an iPhone 16 purchase does not guarantee immediate access to every Apple Intelligence feature in every market.

What This Means for You

The event's clear throughline is Apple Intelligence, nearly every hardware improvement this cycle, from the new chip to the Camera Control button, is framed around enabling on-device AI features. If Apple Intelligence is a priority for you, the iPhone 16 is effectively the entry point, since older iPhones lack the neural processing power to run it. If you don't care about Apple Intelligence, the practical upgrade case is weaker, the Camera Control button and AirPods 4 ANC are the main tangible changes for everyday use.

What Happens Next

Preorders opened in the days following the announcement, with devices reaching stores shortly after. Apple Intelligence features will roll out via a staged software update rather than arriving all at once on day one, CodeNexon will publish a follow-up once we can confirm real-world availability and performance firsthand.

What's Still Unconfirmed

Apple has not published independently verified battery-life or camera benchmarks beyond its own marketing claims; those figures above are Apple's stated numbers, not measurements CodeNexon has confirmed firsthand. Exact regional pricing and carrier-specific offers were also not covered in this recap, check Apple's official store for your region before buying.

FAQs

Does the base iPhone 16 support Apple Intelligence?
Yes. The A18 chip in the standard iPhone 16 supports Apple Intelligence, a first for a non-Pro iPhone.

Do the new AirPods 4 replace the AirPods Pro?
No. The AirPods Pro remain Apple's top-tier earbuds; the ANC AirPods 4 variant sits between the standard AirPods and the Pro line.

Is sleep apnea detection a medical diagnosis?
No. It flags signs of moderate to severe sleep apnea for you to discuss with a doctor, not a standalone diagnosis.
