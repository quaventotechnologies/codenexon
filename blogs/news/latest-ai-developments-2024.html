Latest AI Developments in 2024: What You Need to Know

GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and the EU AI Act, the AI releases and regulatory shifts that mattered most in 2024, explained.

Shubham Handore Published 10 Sep, 2026 ~5 min read

This is a roundup and analysis of releases that happened over several months in 2024, not a single news event, individual release dates are noted in each section below.

2024 has been a landmark year for AI. New models from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic pushed capabilities further than ever, while regulators began catching up with real rules. Here's what mattered most.

Quick answer: The biggest 2024 AI developments were GPT-4o's multimodal free access, Gemini 1.5 Pro's 1-million-token context window, Claude 3.5 Sonnet's coding strength, and the EU AI Act becoming law.

OpenAI GPT-4o

GPT-4o ("Omni") processes text, audio, and images natively within a single model, rather than stitching together separate systems for each input type. This enables real-time voice conversation with visual understanding, you can show it something through your camera and talk about it naturally, with response latency OpenAI reports as close to human conversation speed. Notably, GPT-4o was made available free to ChatGPT users, not locked behind the paid tier, significantly expanding access to frontier AI capability.

Google Gemini 1.5 Pro

Gemini 1.5 Pro introduced a 1-million-token context window, the largest publicly available at the time, dwarfing the context windows most competing models offered. In practice, this means Gemini can process an entire codebase, a full-length novel, or hours of video transcript in a single query without needing to chunk or summarize the input first, enabling use cases like whole-repository code analysis that were previously impractical.

Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet

Claude 3.5 Sonnet became a favorite among many developers for its exceptional coding ability, strong instruction-following, and more predictable, less "chatty" behavior compared to competitors, it tends to do what's asked without excessive hedging or unnecessary preamble. Its Artifacts feature, which renders code and content in a separate interactive pane, also changed how many developers use AI assistants for iterative building rather than just one-off Q&A.

The EU AI Act

Beyond model releases, 2024 was also the year AI regulation became concrete rather than theoretical. The EU AI Act became law, establishing the world's first comprehensive AI regulatory framework, built around a risk-based classification system, applications are categorized from minimal risk to unacceptable risk, with obligations scaling accordingly. This sets a regulatory template other jurisdictions are likely to reference as they draft their own AI legislation.

Availability and Who's Affected

GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 Pro are both broadly available today through their respective consumer apps and APIs, though some Gemini 1.5 Pro capabilities (like the full 1-million-token window) have been gated to paid tiers or limited-access programs at various points. Claude 3.5 Sonnet is available through Anthropic's consumer apps and API in supported regions. The EU AI Act affects any organization offering AI systems to users in the EU, regardless of where the company is based, with its obligations phasing in over several years rather than all at once, companies outside the EU should not assume it doesn't apply to them.

What Happens Next

The pace of progress across all three major labs shows no sign of slowing, and competition between OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic continues to drive rapid price drops alongside capability improvements, the same tier of AI capability keeps getting cheaper and more accessible each year. EU AI Act obligations will continue phasing in over the coming years, and CodeNexon will cover the compliance deadlines as they approach. See our generative AI explainer and ChatGPT vs Gemini comparison for a deeper look at how these tools actually stack up in practice.

What's Still Unconfirmed

Response-speed and capability comparisons above draw on each company's own published claims and widely reported developer feedback, not independent benchmarks run by CodeNexon. The EU AI Act's exact enforcement timeline for smaller organizations was still being clarified as of publication, check the European Commission's official guidance for the current schedule.

FAQs

Is GPT-4o free to use?
Yes. OpenAI made GPT-4o available to free ChatGPT users, though with lower usage limits than the paid Plus tier.

What is the EU AI Act's risk-based system?
It classifies AI applications from minimal to unacceptable risk, with compliance obligations scaling to match, higher-risk uses face stricter requirements.

Which model is best for coding?
Claude 3.5 Sonnet is widely regarded as strongest for coding tasks; see our ChatGPT vs Gemini comparison for how it stacks up against the others.
