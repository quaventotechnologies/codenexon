Best VPN Services of 2024: Honest Comparison

Mullvad, ProtonVPN, and ExpressVPN compared on privacy, price, and speed, based on published audits and independently reported testing, to help you pick the right one.

Shubham Handore Published 05 Sep, 2026 ~5 min read

A VPN encrypts your internet traffic and masks your IP address, making it harder for your ISP, network operators, or snoopers on public Wi-Fi to see what you're doing online. Here's what actually matters when choosing one.

Quick answer: Mullvad for maximum anonymity, ProtonVPN for the best free tier, ExpressVPN if streaming access is your priority and budget isn't.

What to Look For

Not all VPNs are equal, and marketing claims are easy to make and hard to verify. Focus on: a verified, independently audited no-logs policy (not just a promise on the website); jurisdiction (providers based outside the "5 Eyes/9 Eyes/14 Eyes" intelligence-sharing alliances face fewer legal pressures to hand over data); real-world speed; server count and locations; and transparent pricing. Be especially wary of free VPNs, running server infrastructure costs money, and a service that isn't charging you is often monetizing your browsing data instead.

Mullvad VPN, Best for Privacy

Mullvad is built around anonymity from the ground up: it accepts cash and cryptocurrency payments, doesn't require an email address to sign up (you get a randomly generated account number instead), and has undergone multiple independent audits confirming its no-logs claims. It charges a flat €5/month regardless of plan length, no confusing multi-year discount tiers. Based in Sweden, outside the core 5-Eyes alliance.

ProtonVPN, Best Free Option

ProtonVPN, from the team behind Proton Mail, offers a genuinely unlimited free tier with no data caps, rare among free VPNs, most of which throttle speed or cap monthly usage. It's Switzerland-based, open source (so its code can be independently reviewed), and has a proven no-logs track record. The free tier is limited to a handful of server locations, but for basic privacy and public Wi-Fi protection, it's hard to beat at zero cost.

ExpressVPN, Best for Streaming

ExpressVPN is reported by independent testers to post some of the fastest speeds among major VPNs and reliably unblock geo-restricted content on Netflix, Disney+, and other major streaming platforms, services that actively try to detect and block VPN traffic. It's noticeably more expensive than Mullvad or ProtonVPN, but for anyone specifically prioritizing streaming access alongside privacy, the reliability difference is worth the price.

Choosing Between Them

If your priority is pure anonymity and you don't mind a slightly more technical signup process, go with Mullvad. If you want a capable free option with no strings attached, start with ProtonVPN. If streaming access is your main use case and budget isn't the deciding factor, ExpressVPN is the safer bet.

FAQs

Is a free VPN ever safe to use?
ProtonVPN's free tier is a rare exception with a genuine no-logs track record; most other free VPNs monetize your data, so stick to well-audited options.

Will a VPN slow down my internet?
Some speed reduction is normal since traffic routes through an extra server, but reputable providers keep the impact minimal on typical browsing.

Is using a VPN legal?
Yes in most countries, including India, the US, and the EU, a small number of countries restrict or ban VPN use, so check local laws if traveling.
